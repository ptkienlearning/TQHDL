Five signals worth noticing

PROFITABILITY GAP

Electronics sells the most.
Fashion earns the most.

Electronics leads revenue, but Fashion contributes the highest total profit and has a much stronger margin. Sales scale alone is an incomplete measure of category performance.

STORE EFFICIENCY

District 1 leads sales, but lags on margin.

District 1 has the highest revenue and most transactions, yet its 21.5% margin is the lowest among the four stores. Thu Duc records the highest total profit.

03
DISCOUNT WATCH

Deep discounts coincide with thinner margins.

Transactions discounted above 15% have a 13.9% aggregate margin, compared with 32.0% for discounts of 0–5%. This is an association, not proof that discounts alone caused the difference.

−18.1 ppMargin gap between
deep and light discounts

04
TIME & MOMENTUM

November is the revenue peak; April is the trough.

November generates 22.17M in revenue, while April records 7.71M. Monthly differences may reflect transaction volume and basket size; one year is not enough to establish recurring seasonality.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Longer delivery is associated with lower ratings.

Average rating declines as delivery time increases: approximately 4.51 for 1-day deliveries and 3.63 for 4-day deliveries. Missing ratings are excluded; this pattern is correlational.
